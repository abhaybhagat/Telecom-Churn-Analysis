Customer Churn Analysis Summary

The analysis of the customer churn dataset reveals significant patterns in service usage and its impact on churn behavior. Below are the key takeaways:
Churn Trends by Service:


Customers without essential services such as Online Security and Tech Support exhibit higher churn rates. Specifically, 42% of customers without Online Security and 45% without Tech Support are likely to churn, compared to 15-20% for those who utilize these services.
Streaming services (TV and Movies) have a smaller impact on churn, with churn rates of approximately 30% for customers who opt out of these services versus 25% for those who use them.
Internet Service Type:


Fiber optic users experience the highest churn rates at 41%, compared to 23% for DSL users and 10% for customers with no internet service. This highlights potential dissatisfaction or cost concerns among fiber optic users.
Phone and Multiple Line Services:


Customers with no phone service have a churn rate of 25%, while those with a single-line plan show a churn rate of 30%. In contrast, customers with multiple lines exhibit a churn rate of 38%, suggesting possible dissatisfaction with multi-line plans.
Tenure and Monthly Charges:
Customers with a tenure of less than one year have a churn rate exceeding 60%, indicating that early retention strategies are critical. Conversely, customers with a tenure of more than five years have a churn rate of only 10%.
Monthly charges also influence churn. Customers paying over $80 per month have a churn rate of 50%, while those paying less than $50 exhibit a churn rate of 15%.
Recommendations:


Enhance online security and tech support services to retain customers. Target customers without these services with promotional offers or improved features.
Investigate and address issues specific to fiber optic internet, such as pricing or service quality, to reduce churn.
Evaluate multi-line service plans to understand their relationship with higher churn rates and adjust offerings accordingly.
Develop retention strategies focused on new customers, especially within the first year of service, to improve long-term loyalty.
Reassess pricing strategies, particularly for customers with higher monthly charges, to make services more appealing and reduce churn.
This analysis underscores the importance of tailoring services and addressing pain points to improve customer retention and reduce churn effectively.
